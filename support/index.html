Support

How can we help?

Email support@wellforge.app or send a note with the form. Typical replies cover logging, steps, backups, and store installs.

Before you write

  • Food not in the list: search by brand, scan a barcode, or create a custom food.
  • Steps stuck at zero: Profile → Health / steps → allow activity permission, or type today’s count.
  • Want a clean slate: export a backup first, then Profile → Delete all local data.

We do not provide medical advice. For diet or training changes, talk with your physician.
